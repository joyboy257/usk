## 2. Read Tone Signals

Review the ticket's language. Look for frustration, urgency, legal threats, or mentions of churn.

Tone level indicators:
- 🔴 Critical: legal threats, executive cc'd, "cancel" or "churn" mentioned
- 🟡 Elevated: frustration markers, repeated follow-ups, "urgent" in subject
- 🟢 Normal: standard support language, no emotional markers
