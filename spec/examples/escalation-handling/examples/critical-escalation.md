## Example: Critical Production Outage

**Customer:** Enterprise Corp (Tier 1, $1.2M ARR)
**Issue:** All users unable to access the platform (HTTP 503)
**Duration:** 45 minutes and counting

**Tone signals:**
- "Our entire company is blocked"
- "This is completely unacceptable"
- "We need someone on the phone NOW"

**Assessment:**
- **Impact:** Critical — revenue-impacting production outage
- **Root cause:** Unknown (requires immediate engineering investigation)
- **History:** First major outage for this account

**Response:**
1. Acknowledge within 15 minutes
2. Escalate to on-call engineering immediately
3. Provide public status page update
4. Schedule 30-min check-in cadence until resolved
5. Prepare post-mortem template for after resolution
