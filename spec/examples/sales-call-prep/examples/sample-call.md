## Example: Mid-Enterprise Renewal Call

**Account:** Acme Corp (500 seats, ARR $240K)
**Stage:** Renewal (90 days out)
**Stakeholders:** CIO (economic), Head of Eng (evaluator), Procurement (blocker)

**Signals:**
- Last two QBRs had declining NPS from the engineering team
- CIO mentioned "evaluating alternatives" in the last exec briefing
- A competitor's case study was shared internally two weeks ago

**Questions to ask:**
1. What has changed in your priorities since we last met?
2. How is the current solution meeting your team's needs?
3. What would need to be true for you to renew for another two years?
